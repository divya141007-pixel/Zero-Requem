How it works

Three steps take a handmade product from a phone photo to a globally discoverable listing.

Scan

The seller photographs the artifact. No studio setup needed — one clear photo is enough to start.

02

Know

Zero Requem drafts the listing and benchmarks a fair price range against real handicraft-marketplace data.

03

Connect

The listing is matched to the best-fit marketplaces and published to the shared buyer catalog for discovery.
